Policies

Shipping, payment and inspection

These terms apply to every purchase from The Luxe Corner LLC.

Shipping

Every order ships fully insured for its full purchase price, and delivery requires a signature.

Armored delivery is available on request. We quote its cost in advance, and the buyer pays it.

Payment

We accept wire transfer and credit card.

Credit card payment is accepted for purchases up to $150,000. Purchases above $150,000 must be paid by wire transfer.

Three-day inspection window

You may inspect your piece for 3 days from the date of delivery. To return it, send us a message on Instagram within those 3 days.

  • The piece must be returned unworn.
  • The buyer pays for return shipping and insurance.
  • Refunds are issued within 7 to 10 business days, once we have received and inspected the returned piece.

Condition and photographs

Condition descriptions are our good-faith assessment of each piece. The photographs on every listing show the actual piece offered.

Pricing and availability

Prices and availability may change without notice, and every piece is subject to prior sale.
