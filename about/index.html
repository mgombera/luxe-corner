About

A collector's corner

The Luxe Corner began with a personal collection and a habit of looking closely: at dials, at movements, at the details that separate a good watch from an exceptional one.

We keep the selection small on purpose. Every piece here is one we would gladly wear ourselves, and every conversation is handled personally, from first question to delivery.

Alongside the great houses we carry the work of independent watchmakers, whose craft and vision we admire just as much.
